# Fixit project blueprint

**Business:** Fixit, a mobile repair and accessories shop in Kamoki, Punjab, Pakistan. Founder: Ali Hassan. Currency PKR (whole rupees). English first; the structure leaves room for Urdu / Roman Urdu.

**Priorities:** (1) qualified orders and repair requests, (2) fast, trustworthy mobile shopping, (3) repeat purchases, (4) local credibility, (5) organic search, (6) low cost and complexity, (7) room to grow.

**Principles:** never invent stock, prices, reviews, warranties, repair success rates, delivery promises, addresses or phone numbers; unknown facts show "to be confirmed"; the server decides prices and stock; no fake payments, emails or analytics.

## Customer-facing
Home · Shop (search, filters, sort) · Category pages · Product pages · Cart · Guest checkout (COD or bank transfer) · Order confirmation and tracking · Repair request and status · Contact (form, WhatsApp, map) · About (optional founder section) · FAQ · Shipping, returns, warranty, privacy, terms · Buying guides (blog) · Optional accounts (orders, addresses, wishlist, reviews) · Custom 404, error and maintenance pages.

## Staff-facing (role based)
Dashboard with real numbers · Orders and status workflow · Repair requests · Contact messages · Products, categories, inventory · Coupons · Delivery zones and fees · Blog · Review moderation · Customers · Email list export · Store settings · Payments status · Email status · Staff accounts · Audit log.

## Retention
Recently viewed, compatible accessories, order again, saved addresses, wishlist, verified reviews, tracking, consent-based email list. Not built: campaigns, back-in-stock alerts, loyalty.

## Quality bar
Phase complete only when deliverables exist, tests were run, defects are fixed and limits are documented. Features awaiting credentials are "implemented but awaiting configuration".
